A multimodal framework for detecting and explaining hate speech in memes. Rather than classifying the image directly, a retrieval-augmented pipeline (ClipCap, BLIP, EasyOCR, entity extraction and web-context retrieval) builds a context-aware caption, and a T5 model with chain-of-thought prompting writes out why the meme is hateful. On the HatReD dataset it reached BLEU 0.212 and ROUGE-L 0.401, ahead of the strongest text-only baseline. Published in the Companion Proceedings of the ACM Web Conference 2025, Sydney.
